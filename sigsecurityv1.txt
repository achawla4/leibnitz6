Tthere is a growing field that blends **signal processing** with **cybersecurity**. It doesn’t always go by a single formal name, but researchers often describe it as **signal processing for security**, **statistical signal analysis in intrusion detection**, or **spectral methods for anomaly detection**.  

---

## 🔐 How Signal Processing Helps Cybersecurity

### 1. Frequency-Domain Analysis
- **Idea:** A signal that looks normal in the time domain may reveal hidden periodicities or distortions in the frequency domain.  
- **Use Case:** Detecting covert channels or malware traffic that embeds patterns invisible to packet timing alone.  

---

### 2. Wavelet & Time-Frequency Methods
- **Idea:** Wavelet transforms capture both time and frequency information.  
- **Use Case:** Spotting short-lived anomalies in encrypted traffic or denial-of-service bursts.  

---

### 3. Statistical Signal Models
- **Idea:** Treat network traffic or sensor data as stochastic processes.  
- **Use Case:** Intrusion detection systems use **spectral density estimation** to flag deviations from baseline behavior.  

---

### 4. Adaptive Filtering
- **Idea:** Filters can adapt to evolving threats by continuously updating coefficients.  
- **Use Case:** Noise suppression in wireless security systems, or filtering out spoofed signals in IoT networks.  

---

### 5. Cross-Domain Fusion
- **Idea:** Combine time-domain packet inspection with frequency-domain traffic analysis.  
- **Use Case:** Detecting advanced persistent threats that mimic normal traffic but leave subtle spectral fingerprints.  

---

## 📊 Example Analogy
- **Time domain:** Looking at a heartbeat waveform — it may look regular.  
- **Frequency domain:** A Fourier transform might reveal abnormal harmonics, signaling arrhythmia.  
- **Cyber analogy:** Normal-looking traffic may hide periodic beaconing to a command-and-control server, detectable only in frequency space.  

---

In fact, IEEE has entire tracks on **signal processing in network security**, and DARPA has funded projects using **spectral analysis for malware detection**.
